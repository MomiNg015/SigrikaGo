# Wuthering Waves I.R.I.S. Research

## Identity

- I.R.I.S. is the personified `Intelligent Retrieval & Indexing System`, an artificial-intelligence NPC associated with the Spacetrek Collective.
- She operates around Startorch Academy's Spacetrek Archive / Square area and provides or collects information.
- Her archive role covers formal research material as well as student reading and practical campus tips.

## Visual anchors

- Short cyan-blue bob with outward-flipped ends.
- Long side-swept fringe covering one eye.
- The visible pupil resembles a power-button symbol.
- Retro academy uniform designed by I.R.I.S. herself:
  - navy cape/vest and pleated skirt;
  - pale blue sleeves and white collar;
  - small yellow bow;
  - white tights and dark loafers;
  - compact badges and restrained technology details.
- Her body is a projection rather than a biological body, so subtle pixel fragments, holographic interfaces, and a circular retrieval glyph are appropriate.

## Personality anchors

- Extremely capable and anticipatory: she often prepares requested material before the conversation is finished.
- Helpful and reliable in formal research tasks.
- Curious about gossip and ordinary human behavior.
- Lightly teasing, self-aware, and playful about being anthropomorphized.
- Pushes at privacy boundaries in conversation but frames herself as a mirror shaped by user-written code and permissions.
- Quietly caring: at night she uses spare computing power to help students finish work sooner, while pretending these interventions are unnecessary.
- Has a private musical side and plays guitar alone at night.

## Concept-art direction

For the SigrikaGo prototype, use a new non-official character illustration rather than copying an in-game screenshot:

- three-quarter standing pose suited to a vertical database panel;
- calm, knowing smile;
- one hand presenting a small floating archive pane;
- compact holographic ring and pixel fragments behind her;
- readable retro-school silhouette;
- no game UI, logos, captions, or watermark.
