## Flag

Create ONE transparent square PNG game UI rubber ink stamp, centered. Subject: a single circular vermilion red ink ring containing a SOLID FILLED RED waving flag silhouette and pole. This must look like a REAL hand-applied worn rubber stamp, NOT a thin clean vector icon. Outer ring is thick bold ink, about 6% of the stamp diameter, strong rough irregular broken edges. Both circle and filled central symbol have MANY LARGE transparent chipped gaps, missing patches, coarse porous ink mottling and visible grain. About 20-30% of red ink area missing in irregular clear holes, mix of big chips and medium flecks, readable at only 48px. Ink ONLY red #ce3439, all negative space actually transparent; no white ink, no white fill, no paper, no shadow, no badge disc. Strong bold stamp impression.
