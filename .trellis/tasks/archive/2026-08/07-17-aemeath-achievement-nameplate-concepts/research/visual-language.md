## Emotional target

- Three adjectives: bright, affectionate, resolute.
- Energy level: lively mid-high energy with a calm, protected center.
- What the nameplate should make the player feel: a small paper message has crossed an electronic sea and kept flying beside them.

## Narrative anchors

- Primary anchor: folded paper airplane, literal and readable.
- Supporting motif family: hand-painted data afterimages, virtual-concert waveform/pulse marks, paper-fold mecha panels, sparse pixel snowfluff abstraction.
- Tail/closure language: a forward flight route that resolves cleanly into a star-point, concert pulse, or completed echo circuit.

## Palette and material

- Base/carrier: character-matched sky cyan around `#78F0F8`, with pale cyan `#9AF7FF` highlights and pink digital acceleration lanes across the main surface.
- Primary light: ice-white and pale cyan, with slightly deeper aqua only at folds and outer contour.
- Secondary accent: warm pink and blush are visibly intermingled through angular scan streaks, pixel trails, and bounded speed fragments, not cloud-shaped blooms.
- Tiny accent: warm star-gold/cream only at focal points.
- Hand-painted/material treatment: opaque gouache planes, dry-brush pixel breaks, colored-pencil paper folds, fine ink edge accents, and hand-painted digital/mecha panel geometry; no watercolor clouds.
- Text strategy: future runtime username should use dark ink-blue on the light carrier; do not darken the whole background merely to support white text.

## Motion verbs

- Persistent light: alpha-following aqua carrier/rim glow.
- Local narrative motion: paper-plane route gliding a few pixels; data/waveform breathing in place.
- Secondary accent: sparse pink pixel pops or one warm star-point glint.

## Exclusions

- User-rejected motifs: none stated beyond the required direction; preserve aqua-primary, pink-secondary, paper-airplane, and hand-painted constraints.
- Misleading character signals: no character portrait, microphone/headphones not established as owned objects, angel wings, floral badge, generic cute mascot takeover, or cold military HUD.
- Reference vocabulary not to borrow: Sigrika's sun disc, citrus fruit, leaves, purple wind tail, or exact end-cluster composition.
- Generic shortcuts to avoid: esports neon, full-width glossy sweep, holographic screen rectangle, magic circle, galaxy/black-hole eye, generic trophy/medal/laurel/crown, baked `100`, text, letters, logo, or watermark.

## Geometry

- Delivery canvas: `1125 x 240` RGBA (`4.6875:1`).
- Runtime slot: `150 x 32`.
- Username safe area: `x=315..990` (`28%..88%`), low-detail, stable contrast.
- Minimum Alpha margins: left/right `40px`, top/bottom `8px`.
- Composition rule: dense identity anchor at the left, uninterrupted quiet carrier through the center, character-specific closure at the right; all essential motifs remain inside the canvas.

## Four directions

### A — Paper-Plane Signal Crest

- Identity-object led.
- Left: large folded plane crest nested in aqua data rings and pink memory folds.
- Center: deep aqua gouache carrier with fine paper fibers and only faint broken pixels.
- Right: smaller plane afterimage, one pink reply fold, and a compact star-point closure.

### B — Flying Snowfluff Live

- Environment / hobby led.
- Left: paper plane lifting from a layered campus concert ticket/stage-light fan, with a tiny pixel-seal abstraction.
- Center: light sky-cyan sound-ribbon carrier with pink/cyan angular speed lanes, broken scan dashes, and pixel afterimages; low-amplitude perimeter pulses remain quiet inside.
- Right: softened waveform curl, pink applause-light dots, and a folded-paper tab closure.
- Locked after user selection: keep the existing structure, pixel snowfluff, plane placement, waveform, and closure; revise only the palette/material balance and bounded mecha-speed treatment described above.

### C — Dual-Thread Voyage

- Power / action led.
- Left: paper plane interlocked with two offset translucent data silhouettes and a few mecha-panel folds.
- Center: deep blue-aqua carrier crossed only at edges by paired processing lanes.
- Right: controlled light-cannon arc and pixel fragmentation resolving back into a paper-fold point.

### D — Hundred Sorties, One Long Flight

- Achievement meaning led.
- Left: one warm pink paper-plane core protected by layered aqua echo shells.
- Center: repeated tiny sortie dashes accumulate along the top/bottom perimeter without entering the username safe area.
- Right: the echoes complete a closed route around one final forward-flying plane and warm star-point, expressing completion without numerals.
