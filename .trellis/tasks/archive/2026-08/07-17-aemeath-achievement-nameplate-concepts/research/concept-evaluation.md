## A — Paper-Plane Signal Crest

- Primary read: large white paper airplane nested in a circular aqua signal crest.
- Supporting read: pink memory-paper folds, small plane afterimage, warm star points.
- Runtime read: clearest literal paper-airplane identity and strongest emblem-like left anchor.
- Structural difference: round left crest plus compact reply-flight closure.

## B — Flying Snowfluff Live

- Primary read: paper airplane launching from layered concert-light / campus-paper fans.
- Supporting read: tiny pixel snowfluff abstraction, perimeter waveform, pink audience lights.
- Runtime read: most playful and idol-specific; the waveform remains present without interrupting the username.
- Structural difference: fan-shaped left environment and musical S-curve closure.

## C — Dual-Thread Voyage

- Primary read: stacked paper-airplane formation and translucent processing afterimages.
- Supporting read: angular mecha-panel folds, paired border lanes, cyan action burst with pink data fragments.
- Runtime read: fastest and most combat-forward silhouette; still reads as handmade paper/data rather than an esports badge.
- Structural difference: sharp horizontal launch mass and right-side action impact.

## D — Hundred Sorties, One Long Flight

- Primary read: pink paper airplane protected by layered aqua route shells.
- Supporting read: repeated tiny perimeter sorties and a completed flight path around the final plane.
- Runtime read: strongest achievement/persistence metaphor and most balanced two-ended composition.
- Structural difference: closed continuous journey frame with repeated route marks rather than a separate impact or concert cluster.
