# Dialogue diagnosis

## Diagnosed states

- D1 Identical voices: the previous draft gave normal Sigrika, blackened Sigrika, and the player the same polished argumentative register.
- D2 Wooden dialogue: many lines were complete thesis statements with balanced syntax and few corrections, fragments, or evasions.
- D4 No subtext: characters named the theme and their conclusions instead of revealing fear through what they avoided.
- D5 Single-function dialogue: most exchanges existed only to explain one of the four propositions; relationship and power rarely shifted inside an exchange.
- D6 Pacing mismatch: the dream confrontation used long explanations even when the scene should tighten and become harder to answer.
- Anti-patterns: The Philosopher and The Tennis Match were both present.

## Layer analysis

- Text: abstract nouns, parallel constructions, and aphorisms made lines sound written instead of spoken.
- Subtext: normal Sigrika said exactly what she felt; blackened Sigrika argued instead of tempting; the player answered too confidently.
- Context: narration described gestures but the gesture seldom altered the next line or exposed a relationship need.

## Voice rules

### Normal Sigrika

- Bright, quick, and concrete. She notices pages, fingers, candy sounds, the board, and another person's face before she names an idea.
- Uses short turns, self-corrections, small jokes, and questions. When hurt, she circles around the admission and tries to soften it.
- Does not call the player `前辈`. Use `{username}` only for an urgent wake-up call.
- Never finishes an argument for the reader. Her emotional tell is that she stops turning pages, leaves candy untouched, or keeps holding a page corner.

### Blackened Sigrika

- The player's avoidance speaking back in Sigrika's shape. Quiet and intimate rather than theatrical.
- Uses questions, repetition, and an easier-looking exit: put the hand down, do not start, keep the untested possibility intact.
- Short lines dominate. Examples from the manga are allowed before the decisive battle, but the voice turns directly toward the player after it.
- Does not insult. Even in defeat, she sounds almost considerate, which makes the temptation more dangerous.

### Player

- Personal, brief, and imperfect. The player does not deliver essays or prove the world fair.
- Admits pain, limitation, and uncertainty before asserting agency.
- Four victory answers use two different tones per round: one boundary-setting, one vulnerable; both converge immediately.

### Danya

- Dry, economical, practical humor. She relieves pressure after waking without explaining the theme.

## Interventions applied

- Replace thesis speeches with object-led beats and lines that do two jobs: reveal a stance and shift power.
- Use silence and interrupted phrasing selectively, not as decoration.
- Make blackened Sigrika tempt retreat instead of lecturing about nihilism.
- Make the final emotional proof an ordinary resumed activity, not a concluding slogan.
