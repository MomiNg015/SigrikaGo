## Alternatives explored

### Dream antagonist

### Victory landing

A. A direct argument that effort creates meaning.

B. A promise to continue until success.

C. A smaller claim: no guaranteed outcome, but the player keeps authority over whether to make the next move.

Selected: C. It answers the writer's actual fear without pretending the world is fair.

## Prose strategy

- Give each reading scene one tactile anchor: page corner, black stone dust, rain on a terminal window, damp sleeve, lantern reflection.
- Let page-turning speed reveal emotion.
- Save the most explicit negative theses for the dream; normal Sigrika only senses the wounds.
- Use silence and interrupted gestures at emotional peaks.
- End on an ordinary sound, not a slogan.
