# 达妮娅用户名背景三版提示词

## Shared execution notes

- Built-in ImageGen, one independent generation per direction.
- Use case: `stylized-concept`.
- Output only one isolated, orthographic, extremely wide game username nameplate on a perfectly flat `#00ff00` chroma-key field.
- The chroma field is a removable working background, not part of the art. No green may appear in the subject.
- Candidate art is later fitted uniformly into `1125 x 240`; no left/core/right independent stretching.
- Existing production Danya asset is not an edit target and is not overwritten.
