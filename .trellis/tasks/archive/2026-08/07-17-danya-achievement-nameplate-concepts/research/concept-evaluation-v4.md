# Danya reference-guided concept evaluation

## Shared checks

- Delivery canvas: all four files are exact `1125 x 240` RGBA PNGs.
- Runtime check: all four were downsampled to `150 x 32` and inspected with the representative username `Moming`.
- Asset structure: transparent outside silhouette, decoration concentrated at the ends, uninterrupted center carrier.
- Character language: bubbles lead; the album/page cue stays secondary; pink dominates; dark purple supports depth and text contrast.
- Rejected language: no doll, plush, fabric, patchwork, stitches, sewing, or mending imagery.
- Production isolation: no achievement seed, production raster, CSS owner, or runtime code was changed.

## A — Bubble Crest

- Silhouette: asymmetrical bubble cluster on the left; small album end cap on the right.
- Runtime read: the clustered left mass remains distinct; center carrier stays quiet.
- Tradeoff: most playful and character-object-led of the four.

## B — Bubble Ribbon

- Silhouette: balanced large bubble discs at both ends with a curved carrier.
- Runtime read: strongest symmetry and clearest conventional nameplate outline.
- Tradeoff: least album-forward; the album tab is intentionally only a punctuation mark.

## C — Album Window

- Silhouette: compact left bubble cluster plus album corner; large bubble ring at the right.
- Runtime read: dark-purple inner band gives the strongest username contrast.
- Tradeoff: visually densest of the set, while still resolving as a few chunky forms.

## D — Protective Orbit

- Silhouette: pale carrier riding a broad purple orbit with three attached bubble discs.
- Runtime read: lightest and calmest carrier; bubbles remain legible at the ends.
- Tradeoff: dark purple is most clearly an understructure rather than a text field.
