# Danya nameplate visual language

## Emotional target

- Three adjectives: dreamy, guarded, quietly hopeful.
- Energy level: low and drifting at rest, with one contained moment of resolve.
- Player feeling: long familiarity with a teasing companion whose fragile heart has repeatedly chosen to protect others.

## Narrative anchors

- Primary anchor: a protected echo bubble or compact bubble cluster.
- Supporting motif family: a single simplified album/page corner, void-colored underlayer, or repeated protected lights.
- Tail/closure language: tapered bubble trail, photo-paper corner, or a completed circular pulse; never a generic glossy sweep.

## Palette and material

- Base/carrier: strawberry pink, rose quartz, pale blush, cream, and softly luminous lilac; the carrier should read pink and bright even at runtime height.
- Primary light: warm pearl white, candy pink, and candle peach.
- Secondary accent: a deliberate dark-purple or deep-plum underlayer for depth, framing, and username contrast, with tiny blue-and-gold jewelry notes.
- Hand-painted/material treatment: opaque gouache carrier with restrained watercolor blooms, dry-brush edges, visible pigment pooling, and no polished vector finish.

## Motion verbs for the later selected asset

- Persistent light: a contained heart or bubble core breathes without disappearing.
- Local narrative motion: slow buoyant drift, refraction settle, or album-paper lift depending on the selected direction.
- Secondary accent: two or three sparse bubble glints or protected-light pulses.

## Exclusions

- User-rejected motifs: none stated beyond the requirement for hand-painted character specificity.
- Misleading character signals: victorious aggression, royal authority, a cheerful candy-only mascot mood, or allegiance to the Remnant Stars.
- Generic visual shortcuts: full-width glossy sweep, screen haze, rotating neon ring, sparkly esports frame, trophy/medal/laurel, and blink-only lighting.
- User-rejected direction: a mostly dark purple plate.
- User-rejected motif: the repaired doll, stitched fabric, patches, visible mending, sewing thread, or any doll-shaped surrogate.

## Revision 2 shared visual sentence

Every concept tells the same focused story: pink protective echo bubbles hold warm memories against a dark-purple depth, while small album/photo-corner cues suggest Danya’s Star Torch campus belonging. Bubbles own the silhouette and narrative; album elements remain quiet punctuation rather than a competing theme. No doll, fabric, stitching, patchwork, or mending language is permitted.

## Geometry

- Delivery canvas: `1125 x 240` (`4.6875:1`).
- Runtime slot: approximately `150 x 32`, scaled by the consuming surface.
- Username safe area: `x=315..990` (`28%..88%`), low-detail and high-contrast.
- Minimum Alpha margins for the later final asset: start at `40px` left/right and `8px` top/bottom; concepts must already keep essential motifs away from all edges.
- Static composition: identity anchor mainly in the left `0%..28%`, quiet carrier through `28%..88%`, and a small closure in the right `88%..96%`.
