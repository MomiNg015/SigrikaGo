## Evidence-based design vocabulary

The following palette is an original UI-oriented synthesis from the official art rather than a claim that these are official brand tokens:

- Oxblood / deep crimson: `#4A0B18`, `#651225`
- Burgundy curtain midtone: `#7B1B30`
- Black plum / stage shadow: `#18060D`, `#2A0712`
- Antique brass: `#B78943`, `#D3AE67`
- Bone / ivory highlight: `#E8DCC8`
- Controlled spectral accent: `#B99AC8`

Reusable motifs:

- Heavy theater curtains and a proscenium arch.
- Conductor / musical-performance staging without copying a character.
- Bent or fractured brass-instrument shapes used as ornamental hardware.
- Sparse glass shards and red petals along the outer frame and floor.
- Red-and-ivory costume silhouettes on rails or dress forms.
- Faceted triangular metal geometry and mask-like ornaments as secondary faction cues.
- Warm brass footlights plus a restrained cool-white or mauve spotlight.

## Composition contract for this project

Desktop:

- The existing mascot host owns the left `34%` of the shop body.
- The product stage begins around `35%` and uses the remaining right side.
- Keep the right-center stage quiet enough for one to five transparent costume portraits.
- Decorative highlights belong at the perimeter, behind the mascot, or below the product baseline; avoid bright rectangles that resemble controls.

Mobile:

- The product stage owns the upper `57%`.
- The mascot, speech bubble and wallet own the lower `43%`.
- Use a purpose-built portrait composition: upper hanging-rail / spotlight zone, lower dressing-room reception zone.
- Keep the upper center low-detail so two-row product layouts stay legible at both `375x812` and `375x600`.

## Generation guardrails

- Do not reproduce Phrolova, Scar, official portraits, logos or textual marks.
- No people, faces, readable writing, game UI, price tags or button-like panels.
- Match the existing Zahira shop's rough wax-crayon language: chunky strokes, chalky paper grain, uneven hand-drawn outlines, simplified color blocks and visibly imperfect geometry.
- Avoid polished concept-art rendering, photorealism, glossy showroom materials, fine filigree and cinematic micro-lighting.
- Use Fractsidus references as mood, palette and motif evidence only.
