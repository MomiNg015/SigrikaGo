# Reference and repository findings

Research date: 2026-10-19. Visual interpretations below describe inspected screenshots, not every theme or version of either game.

## Arknights
Source: https://prts.wiki/w/个人名片一览
Inspected default preview: https://media.prts.wiki/4/45/个人名片_默认_预览1.png
The default preview places assistant art at large scale on the left, the player block and support units on the right, and medals below. Strong type scale, compact numeric information, cyan labels and a patterned grayscale base create an archival composition. The important lesson is the separation of identity, character display and achievements, rather than simply painting everything black. PRTS notes multiple profile themes and an option to hide the right components to prioritize assistant/background.

## Endfield
Source: https://gamewith.net/akendfield/72406
Inspected image: https://img.gamewith.net/img/original_71536a03db7f6822b48d7c835d538df8.png
The guide screenshot has added yellow/red tutorial annotations: these annotations are not game UI. The actual screen uses a dark upper field and light lower field, a small identity/avatar block on the left, statistics under it, and achievement/operator showcase modules on the right. Thin rules, precise alignment, industrial type and selective yellow accents establish hierarchy. This is a landscape reference, not proof of portrait support.
Additional first-party context: https://endfield.hypergryph.com/news/6094 confirms personal card / operator showcase functionality; it is not the source of pixel-level styling conclusions.

## Fusion decisions
Keep genuine SigrikaGo characters, campus typography, precise labels and current flows. Borrow asymmetrical art/data composition, typographic scale and grouping. Do not borrow game logos, lore, artificial collection metrics or a literal landscape layout. Three directions vary hierarchy and spatial composition, with explicitly separate portrait layouts.
