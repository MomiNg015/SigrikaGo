## Generation prompts

### Student paper

Create a production UI background texture asset, landscape 3:2 ratio, flat front view, full bleed to all edges. A single sheet of beautiful hand-painted Japanese anime school club handbook stationery: warm near-white cotton paper, subtle visible gouache / colored-pencil grain, faint irregular pale warm-grey pencil perimeter line inset 2% from edges, rounded slightly imperfect corners. Delicate muted blue-grey and brown pencil scuffs just along the very outer perimeter. Center 94% completely empty and clean near-white for overlaying functional UI text and character photos. Restrained handcrafted texture, high quality natural scanned illustration finish. No text, no letters, no logos, no symbols, no characters, no spiral rings, no holes, no perspective, no scene, no table, no drop shadow outside. This raster will also be multiplied over pastel character theme colors as a student ID paper layer. Keep it almost white so colored background shows through blend mode. Output only the full-bleed flat paper texture.

### Open book (reference: user's book-entry.png)

Use the attached user's hand-drawn club handbook as the exact visual reference, preserving its homemade colored-pencil/gouache style, warm buttery tan cover, wobbly dark-brown linework, pale yellow squared-paper motif and tiny pastel cyan/mint/pink details. Generate ONE production game UI background asset: this SAME handbook opened to its empty inside pages, viewed straight-on, no perspective tilt. Landscape 3:2 aspect ratio, isolated on truly transparent background with only 2% transparent margin. A broad open book with a narrow folded-back front cover visible along the LEFT 8% (tan cover inside, simple tiny cyan corner sticker, NO characters), a soft brown vertical spine crease at x=9%, and a huge blank near-white warm-paper content area spanning x=13% to 96%, y=6% to 94%. Fine faint squared paper texture at outer edges only, almost imperceptible texture in center. Bottom and right edges show 3-4 hand-drawn layered page edges and a tan backing cover. No metal rings, no spiral binding. Keep almost all of the center empty for runtime student ID cards. NO TEXT, NO LETTERS, NO logo, NO page numbers, NO drawn character cards, NO tab labels, NO tabs protruding (tabs are interactive code separately), NO scenery, NO cast shadow beyond silhouette. This should feel like opening the original illustrated handbook, not a generic blank notebook. Render complete edges unclipped, preserve transparent outside.

Intended displayed size only 150 by 132 pixels. Tiny faint four-point star and small cyan pencil strokes confined to top right corner; very faint pale grey horizontal pencil hatch just in bottom 10%. Center and left 80% clean and empty for an existing character portrait and name to be overlaid by code. IMPORTANT no text, no letters, no names, no numbers, no school headings, no portrait, no photo frame, no buttons, no seals. Quiet, delicate, handcrafted card stock matching a homemade brown-outline club handbook. Nearly white overall so the app can multiply this image over each character's theme color. Avoid decorative clutter or large motifs. Output a single usable flat background, not a mockup or multiple cards.
