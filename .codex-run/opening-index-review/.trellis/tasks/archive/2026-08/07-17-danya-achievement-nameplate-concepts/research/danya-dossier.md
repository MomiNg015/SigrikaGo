## Explicit exclusions

- No generic crowns, medals, laurel wreaths, trophy cups, esports neon, or literal `100`.
- No portrait, face, character silhouette, written name, initials, logos, faction emblems, or UI labels.
- Do not reduce her to pink bubbles, candy, or sleepiness without the void-versus-heart tension.
- Do not borrow Sigrika’s citrus, sun, leaf, or wind-tail vocabulary.
- Do not depict the Remnant Stars as a positive allegiance marker.
- User revision: completely exclude the repaired doll, patchwork, stitching, and mending imagery from the next set.
- User revision: bubbles are the primary identity language; album/campus memories are secondary only.
- User revision: pink must lead the palette, supported by a deliberate dark-purple layer that adds depth without making the plate feel dark overall.
