# Concept prompt record

Mode: built-in `imagegen`, one independent generation per direction. Each prompt used the shared `1125 x 240` geometry, `x=315..990` safe-zone requirement, hand-painted material contract, and no-text/no-logo/no-portrait constraints.

## A — Mended Heart

Generate a flat, very wide character nameplate led by Danya’s repaired doll “Trickster”: a pale pink and lilac stitched crescent shell, worn patchwork, one small heart-bearing bubble, a quiet deep-plum central carrier, and a stitched curl closure. Use gouache, watercolor, pigment pooling, dry-brush edges, and pencil/thread marks. Exclude portrait, text, numerals, logos, victory symbols, generic esports effects, and Sigrika’s citrus/sun/leaf vocabulary.

## B — Afternoon Album

Generate a flat, very wide character nameplate led by Danya’s treasured Star Torch campus memories: cream photo-paper corners without people, a pale pink album edge, lavender bubble lens, afternoon-peach window glow, a calm ink-blue-to-plum paper carrier, and a small pinned torn-paper closure. Use gouache, watercolor wash, dry pastel, colored pencil, and worn paper. Exclude portraits, literal photographs, text, school crests, victory symbols, generic esports effects, and Sigrika motifs.

## C — Bubble-Shadow Threshold

Generate a flat, very wide character nameplate led by `泡影视阈`: one contained dark-violet void bubble with pink/lilac refraction rings, a quiet warm heart core, one partially dissolved virtual-substance fragment, a calm deep-plum liquid-glass carrier, and a controlled inward implosion curl. Use translucent watercolor blooms over opaque gouache with granulating pigment. Exclude portraits, text, black-hole/galaxy clichés, magic circles, victory symbols, generic neon, and Sigrika motifs.

## D — A Hundred Choices

Generate a flat, very wide achievement nameplate where several small protective bubbles carrying warm lights gather into one strawberry-pink heart, continue across a quiet midnight-plum gouache carrier, and finish in one closing stroke and cream spark. Express persistence as repeated choice and protection, without numerals or trophies. Use heartfelt gouache, watercolor, visible brush pressure, pigment edges, and sparse colored pencil. Exclude portraits, text, `100`, medals, laurels, crowns, victory wings, generic stars, esports effects, and Sigrika motifs.
