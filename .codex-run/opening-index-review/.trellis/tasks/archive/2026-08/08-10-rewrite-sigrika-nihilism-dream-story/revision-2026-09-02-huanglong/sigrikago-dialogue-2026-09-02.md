# Dialogue diagnosis

## Main failure states in the previous pass

- D2 wooden dialogue: several lines were complete, balanced propositions rather than something a young person says while reading.
- D4 on-the-nose: normal Sigrika frequently announced the theme instead of reacting to a character or page.
- D5 single-function: blackened Sigrika sometimes only delivered thesis statements; the temptation and her wish to protect the player were not audible beneath them.
- D6 pacing mismatch: emotional peaks had the same exchange length and cadence as ordinary reading scenes.

## Voice notes

### Normal Sigrika

- Fast associative attention: notices a hand, a page corner, an awkward detail, then reaches a feeling.
- Warm but not all-knowing. She can revise herself mid-sentence and admit she is upset.
- Short lively reactions, occasional questions, small sounds such as “唔”“诶”“等一下”; do not overuse.
- Canon voice check: official-story transcripts favor quick observations, earnest self-correction, concrete problem-solving language, and anxiety that turns into “我还可以更快/我一定可以”; keep that vulnerable eagerness without copying dialogue.
- Does not call the player “前辈”. Uses `{username}` only where direct address helps.
- Avoids polished moral summaries. Her positive stance appears in what she hopes a character will do next.

### Blackened Sigrika

- Low volume, little ornament, no insults, no theatrical villain laughter.
- Speaks like someone taking weight off the player's shoulders: “停下就好”“不用证明了”.
- Uses accurate observations and lets the player complete the cruel implication.
- After the battle begins, never invokes the world-internal novel, its characters, tournament, or terminology.
- On defeat, calm certainty. On victory, depleted uncertainty rather than sudden conversion.

### Danya

- Dry, practical, slightly teasing.
- Breaks the emotional pressure without invalidating it.
- Shorter sentences than Sigrika; acts while speaking.

### Player

- Not an essayist. Responses should sound chosen under pressure.
- Victory answers admit fear and limits before asserting agency.
- No promise that effort guarantees improvement, success, visibility, fairness, or meaning.

## Subtext targets

- Reading scenes: Sigrika talks about the book but is also revealing how expectations make her tense.
- Dream invitation: blackened Sigrika says “别做” but means “我不想让你再次受伤”.
- Victory: the player is not teaching a lesson; they are refusing to surrender the decision.
- Wake scene: Sigrika asks about low blood sugar because concern is easier than naming fear; the player asks for a game because action is the answer.

## Anti-pattern watchlist

- Repeated “不是……而是……” constructions.
- Symmetrical aphorisms, three-part slogan lists, abstract nouns stacked together.
- “意义不会消失”“过程同样重要” as unsupported theme statements.
- Every line followed by an explanatory narration beat.
- All characters speaking in complete grammatical sentences.
